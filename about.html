About Us

Our story

History

We are founded in 2014 as a small workshop in Gauteng, Hertiage & Hand Carpentry began with a simpl goal which was to restore the dedication to handcrafted quality in modern furniture. Over the past decade we have expanded from minature repairs to delivering bespoke interior joinery, custom cabinetry, and full structural fitting solutions across South Africa.

Mission

Our mission is to transform living and commmercial spaces through exceptional woodworking. We are to using reliable materials, precise execution, and personal client collaborationto build functional, beautiful woodwork tailored to each client's vision.

Vision

We aim to be South Africa's premier bespoke carpentry brand-setting the benchmark for sustainable craftmanship, design excellence, and lifelong durability in every piece we create.
